    How scoring works

    WPM = correctly typed characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Accuracy = matching characters in your final entry ÷ characters entered × 100. Spaces and punctuation count. Backspace corrections are allowed; the mistake breakdown also remembers corrected errors. No input gives 0 WPM and 0% accuracy and is not saved. Comparisons use the same text mode, round type and chosen duration. Older results without a duration are kept in history but excluded from comparisons.
